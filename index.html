02 · 现场观察

主会场变冷，周边反而更热

我的直观感受：主展馆 Expo Floor 人流明显稀疏，而 Moscone 周围的 Side Event、酒店 Suite Meeting、开发者 Meetup 反倒密集得多。

  • 欧洲、加拿大公司选择减员参会，一些团队甚至完全放弃今年 GDC
  • 差旅成本 + 美国移民政策 + 伊朗局势 共同推高了"不来"的决策
  • 很多商务谈判搬到了场馆外部的 Hotel Suite 与私人活动
  • 官方更名为 "Festival of Gaming"，其实是对这种趋势的主动拥抱
"20,000 人对我来说已经够了。" — Dean Takahashi · GamesBeat 主编
"一次戏剧性的重启，反而做出了一届更聚焦开发者的会议。" — Don Daglow · 游戏行业老兵
我的判断：GDC 的"展会属性"在衰减，"社区属性"在放大。想拿下有用信息，重点不再是抢 Expo 的名片，而是混进对的 Roundtable 和 Side Event。
02 · Floor observations

The main hall went quieter. The periphery got louder.

My direct read: Expo-floor foot traffic was noticeably thin, while side events around Moscone — hotel suites, off-venue meetups, developer meet-ups — were far denser.

  • European and Canadian companies came with smaller delegations; some teams skipped GDC entirely
  • Travel costs + US immigration policy + the Iran situation all pushed the "don't go" decision
  • Many real business meetings moved to hotel suites and private events outside the venue
  • The "Festival of Gaming" rebrand is GDC's own acknowledgment of this shift
"20,000 attendees was enough for me." — Dean Takahashi · Editor, GamesBeat
"A dramatic reboot — and it produced a better, more dev-focused conference." — Don Daglow · Game industry veteran
My read: GDC's "trade-show" DNA is fading; its "community" DNA is growing. If you want real takeaways, stop chasing booth business cards and get into the right roundtables and side events.

03 · My roundtable

100 seats. Every single one taken.

Developers in China · IGDA SZ Meet-up（Presented by the IGDA）

  • 由我作为 IGDA 深圳 会长主持的官方 Roundtable
  • 开场前十分钟，100+ 座位被完全坐满，门口还有人站着听完全场
  • 提问分成两条主线 ——
    ① 如何合法进入中国市场：版号、本地发行、合规；
    ② 如何找到好的中国游戏：外籍开发者反复说"我们找不到"，信息差是最大的墙
  • 参会者背景混合：国内开发者、欧美国外开发者、发行商、学生
我的感受：国际开发者对进入中国市场的渴望远超我出发前的预期。但他们最大的痛点，不是版号也不是本地发行，而是信息差 —— 他们根本不知道哪些中国游戏值得看、该找谁聊。"出海"和"入华"正在同时发生，IGDA 深圳愿意做那座桥。

Developers in China · IGDA SZ Meet-up (Presented by the IGDA)

  • The official roundtable I hosted as Chair of IGDA Shenzhen
  • Within 10 minutes of opening, 100+ seats were completely full, with more people standing at the door for the entire session
  • Questions fell into two main threads ——
    ① How to legally enter the China market: approvals, local publishing, compliance;
    ② How to find good Chinese games: overseas devs kept saying "we can't find them" — information asymmetry is the biggest wall
  • Audience mix: domestic Chinese devs, Western devs, publishers, students
My take: the appetite of international developers to enter China far exceeded what I expected. But their #1 pain point isn't approvals or publishing — it's the information gap. They simply don't know which Chinese games to look at or who to talk to. "Going out" and "coming in" are happening at the same time, and IGDA Shenzhen wants to be that bridge.

07 · Indie developers

Bright spot in the layoffs: indie developers

AAA 被 $100M 预算卡脖子的时候，独立和中型团队反而更抗压 — 这是今年 GDC 最值得记下来的反差。

  • 2026 全行业裁员依旧严重：28% 从业者在过去两年被裁，50% 工作室过去 12 个月进行过裁员
  • 但受冲击最大的是 AAA。独立 / 中小团队因为成本结构轻反而更有韧性
  • 88% 独立开发者完全远程办公 (2023 Indie Game Industry Report) — 全球协作的门槛已经拉平
  • IGF 2026 Grand Prize：Titanium Court — 独立精品依旧能打

While AAA chokes on its $100M+ budgets, indie and mid-size teams are proving more resilient — the most notable contrast of GDC 2026.

  • 2026 industry-wide layoffs remain severe: 28% of professionals laid off in the past 2 years; 50% of studios laid off in the past 12 months
  • The brunt falls on AAA. Indies and smaller teams are more resilient thanks to lighter cost structures
  • 88% of indie devs work fully remote (2023 Indie Game Industry Report) — global collaboration barriers are essentially gone
  • IGF 2026 Grand Prize: Titanium Court — indie quality still delivers

三条 TakeawayThree takeaways

  • 中国市场的吸引力被严重低估。国际开发者不是不想进，是缺一张路线图 — IGDA 深圳愿意做那张路线图。
  • AI 不是明年的事，是今年的事。但全行业的焦虑也在同步放大；小团队反而因为不用等"共识"而跑得更快。
  • 独立开发者的窗口正在打开。AAA 收缩、成本结构轻、远程协作成熟、亚太增速全球最高 — 四条线首次同时对齐。
  • The pull of the China market is badly underestimated. International devs aren't uninterested — they're missing a roadmap. IGDA Shenzhen wants to be that roadmap.
  • AI is not a "next year" topic. It's a this-year topic. Industry anxiety is rising in parallel; small teams run faster precisely because they don't need consensus to move.
  • The indie window is opening. AAA contraction, lighter cost structures, mature remote collaboration, APAC as the fastest-growing region — four lines aligning for the first time.
